U.S. NAEP美国 NAEP

Released mathematics items and answer keys come from the National Assessment of Educational Progress (NAEP), National Center for Education Statistics, Institute of Education Sciences, U.S. Department of Education. The selected source materials are in the U.S. public domain under the NAEP Questions Tool policy.

已公开的数学题与答案来自美国教育部教育科学研究院下属的国家教育统计中心所开展的国家教育进展评估（NAEP）。根据 NAEP 题库的版权政策，所选原始材料在美国属于公有领域。

Math Practice transcribed the released questions and adapted their answer fields for the screen. NAEP supplies the answer keys and the examples credited to its scoring guides. Math Practice wrote the other worked explanations.

数学练习转录了已公开的题目，并调整了屏幕上的答题方式。标准答案及标注为官方评分指南示例的解法来自 NAEP，其余解题过程由数学练习编写。

The Centre for Education in Mathematics and Computing (CEMC) at the University of Waterloo provides the Gauss Contest and Problem of the Week questions, official solutions and included figures. Math Practice reformatted this material and credits any arithmetic correction in the affected solution. Waterloo requires that access to these practice materials remain free of charge.

高斯竞赛与每周一题的题目、官方解答和收录插图由滑铁卢大学数学与计算教育中心（CEMC）提供。数学练习重新排版了这些材料，并在修改过的解答中注明算术更正。滑铁卢大学要求这些练习资源免费开放。
