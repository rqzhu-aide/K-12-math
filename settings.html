Service agreement

One setting controls all three features, independently of your tutor. Recommendations use your practice history whether you type or speak.

Speech-to-text
When you stop recording, Ailleo receives the audio and returns text you can edit before submitting.
Sentiment feedback
When available, Ailleo automatically reads the question and original transcript before you edit or submit it. It comments on clarity and confidence or frustration expressed in words.
Practice recommendations
For suggestions, your browser sends Ailleo’s server the question bank, current question ID, topic or target question, and a limited history of scored question IDs, outcomes (which can include changed-answer retries) and time between visits.

Ailleo temporarily processes recommendation requests, audio, original transcripts and sentiment feedback. Your browser keeps a limited recommendation history. Practice data collection for model training is off. Please do not include any confidential information.
