Your tutor reviews submitted work and gives hints. A request can include your submitted text, the question and any figures, and relevant past attempts and feedback. On the website, requests go directly to the provider you choose.

Ailleo Math Practice Service

Speech-to-text, sentiment feedback and practice recommendations are off by default. You can turn them on or off together in Model settings and keep practicing either way. Speaking is optional.

  • Speech-to-Text LLM. When you stop recording, Ailleo turns your spoken explanation into text you can edit before submitting.
  • Sentiment Check LLM. When available, it automatically reads the question and your original transcript before you edit or submit. It comments on the clarity of your explanation and any confidence or frustration you express in words.

Practice recommendations

For each website recommendation, your browser sends Ailleo the question bank, current question ID, practice topic or target question, and a limited history of scored question IDs, outcomes and gaps between visits. This applies whether you type or speak.

Your first scored answer on each problem helps estimate what you know. If you change your answer and it is scored again, the result can affect today’s suggestions while the tab is open. Several wrong answers may lead to a more manageable problem or a suggestion to pause. A correct answer resets the mistake streak.

Your browser keeps the question bank, question ID, first outcome and day for up to 128 scored problems. Turning the features off clears this recommendation history. Your submitted work stays in your notebook.

How we build recommendations

  1. Study learning over time. We use public math assessments and practice records from different education systems to study how learners’ skills change between visits.
  2. Update as you practice. A Bayesian model starts with a broad estimate, then uses your scored answers to learn where you may be stronger, such as in algebra or geometry.
  3. Test what lasts. We use reinforcement-learning ideas to compare practice sequences with simulated learners. We check what they can still solve weeks later on their chosen topic and across related skills.
  4. Balance learning and strain. We also test whether a more manageable question helps when a learner expresses strain. Silence adds no extra stress penalty. Today, the website uses Sentiment Check for feedback only.

Using the current local launcher

When you run this version locally, the launcher handles recommendation requests. Speech and sentiment use the models you choose in Model settings. Those models can run on your computer or on another server.

If your local installation also uses the Sentiment Check LLM for math assessment, enabling the optional features lets that model check your submitted answers and thinking.

Retention and model training

Ailleo temporarily processes recommendation requests, recordings, original transcripts and sentiment feedback. Practice data collection for model training is off. When you use the website service, Ailleo records your processing choice with an anonymous service session.

Each service you connect, including a local or self-hosted model, follows its operator’s privacy, retention and training policies. Depending on the provider and your account settings, they may retain, review or use submitted text and audio to improve models. Ailleo cannot guarantee how they handle data. The responsible adult should review these policies before enabling an AI feature.

What this browser remembers

In this version, unsubmitted work stays in the open practice tab until you reload or close it. After a valid submission, the browser saves your work in your practice history and notebook. Anyone using the same browser profile may be able to see it.

Opening this version clears earlier autosaved drafts and original speech records while keeping past submissions.

To remove the app's saved browser records, clear this site's data in your browser settings and close its tabs. This also removes saved preferences and this agreement. For copies already held by a service, contact the installation manager or service provider.

Children and learning support

A parent or legal guardian must accept for a child, supervise their use, and help them avoid sharing personal information. Follow each provider’s age limits and terms when choosing services for a child.

AI hints, answers and learning-signal estimates can be wrong. Use them as practice support, check important results, and ask a teacher or guardian when unsure. They are not a formal assessment of a child's ability or wellbeing.
