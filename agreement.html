Privacy & use agreement

An adult must review and accept before practice starts. Children may use Math Practice only under a parent's or legal guardian's supervision.

Please do not include any confidential information.

A voice recording can identify you. Use typed input if you do not want your voice sent to a service.

Math Tutor LLM

Your Math Tutor LLM checks submitted answers and gives hints through the provider you choose. Website requests go directly to that provider under its privacy policy.

When you submit an answer or your thinking, the app can send that text, the question, and relevant previous attempts and feedback to your tutor. You can use the tutor whether the optional features are on or off.

Ailleo Math Practice Service

One optional setting turns on speech-to-text, sentiment feedback and practice recommendations together.

  • Speech-to-Text LLM. When you stop recording, Ailleo turns your spoken explanation into text you can edit before submitting.
  • Sentiment Check LLM. When available, it automatically reads the question and your original transcript before you edit or submit. It comments on the clarity of your explanation and any confidence or frustration you express in words.

Practice recommendations

On the website, Ailleo’s server handles each request for a suggested problem. Your browser sends the question bank, current question ID, practice topic or target question, and a limited history of scored question IDs, outcomes and gaps between visits. This applies even if you always type.

Your first scored answer on each problem helps estimate what you know. If you submit a changed answer and it is marked right or wrong, the result can affect today’s suggestions while the tab stays open. Several wrong answers may lead to a more manageable problem or a suggestion to pause. A correct answer clears the run of mistakes.

Your browser keeps the question bank, question ID, first outcome and day for up to 128 scored problems. Turning the features off clears this recommendation history. Your submitted work stays in your notebook.

How we build recommendations

  1. Study learning over time. We use public math assessments and practice records from different education systems to study how learners’ skills change between visits.
  2. Update as you practice. A Bayesian model starts with a broad estimate, then uses your scored answers to learn where you may be stronger, such as in algebra or geometry.
  3. Test what lasts. We use reinforcement-learning ideas to compare practice sequences with simulated learners. We check what they can still solve weeks later on their chosen topic and across related skills.
  4. Balance learning and strain. We also test whether a more manageable question helps when a learner expresses strain. Silence adds no extra stress penalty. Today, the website uses Sentiment Check for feedback only.

Your choice

Speaking is optional. These features are off until you enable them together in Settings. You can turn these features off and keep practicing.

Using the local app

In the local app, your browser sends the recommendation request to your local launcher. Speech and sentiment use the model connections you choose in Settings. Those models can run on your computer or on another server.

If your local installation also uses the Sentiment Check LLM for math assessment, enabling the optional features lets that model check your submitted answers and thinking.

Retention and model training

Ailleo processes recommendation requests, recordings, original transcripts and sentiment feedback temporarily. Collection of this information for model training is currently off.

When you use the optional Ailleo Math Practice Service on the website, Ailleo records your processing choice with an anonymous service session.

Services you connect yourself follow their own privacy and training policies. They may retain, review or use submitted text and audio to improve models, depending on the provider and your account settings. Ailleo cannot guarantee how those providers handle data.

Before enabling an AI feature, the adult responsible should review the selected services' privacy, retention and training policies. A local or self-hosted model also follows its operator's data practices.

What this browser remembers

In this browser, unsubmitted work stays in the open practice tab. Reloading or closing that tab clears your drafts. After a valid submission, the browser saves your submitted work in your practice history and notebook. Other people using the same browser profile may be able to see saved work.

If you used an earlier version, opening the updated practice page clears its old autosaved drafts and original speech records. Previously submitted work stays in your notebook.

To remove the app's saved browser records, clear this site's data in your browser settings and close its tabs. This also removes saved preferences and this agreement. For copies already held by a service, contact the installation manager or service provider.

Children and learning support

A parent or legal guardian must accept for a child, supervise their use, and help them avoid sharing personal information. Guardian approval does not override a provider's age limits or terms. Only use services permitted for the learner.

AI hints, answers and learning-signal estimates can be wrong. Use them as practice support, check important results, and ask a teacher or guardian when unsure. They are not a formal assessment of a child's ability or wellbeing.
