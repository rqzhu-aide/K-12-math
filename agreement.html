Math Practice

Privacy & use agreement

An adult must review and accept before practice starts. Children may use Math Practice only under a parent's or legal guardian's supervision.

Do not enter or say personal information.

Keep all input about the math. Do not include your name, address, school, contact details, passwords, or other private information about yourself or anyone else.

A voice recording can identify you even without a name. Use typed input if you do not want your voice sent to a service.

What is sent to AI services

Submitting an answer or your thinking can send that text, the question, and relevant previous attempts and feedback to the configured Math Tutor LLM for checks and hints.

Stopping a recording sends the audio to the Speech-to-Text LLM. If the Sentiment Check LLM is configured, the original transcript and question are then sent automatically to that model, before you edit the transcript or press Submit. This analysis estimates expressed confidence, engagement and explanation clarity.

These services may be commercial providers or models running on this computer or another server. The person managing this installation chooses the connections in Settings. Running this interface locally does not mean every model runs locally. A version without AI connections does not send work to models.

Improving question recommendations

With your separate permission, Ailleo will retain your speech transcripts, Sentiment Check LLM results and related question information to train and improve its internal question recommendation model.

When collection is enabled, these records will be kept for up to 90 days (about 3 months). Raw audio recordings will not be retained. Your own Math Tutor LLM's requests and API keys are not part of this collection.

Collection is not enabled yet. Enabling the Speech-to-Text LLM or Sentiment Check LLM currently permits processing only. It does not authorize future collection. When sharing becomes available, you can choose whether to take part and still use these features without sharing.

Deletion requests and privacy questions: rqzhu-aide@qq.com

Retention and model training

Services you connect yourself follow their own privacy and training policies. They may retain, review or use submitted text and audio to improve models, depending on the provider and your account settings. Ailleo cannot guarantee how those providers handle data.

Before enabling an AI feature, the adult responsible should review the selected services' privacy, retention and training policies. A local or self-hosted model also follows its operator's data practices.

What this browser remembers

Drafts, answers, transcripts, practice history and notebook entries are saved in browser storage so you can return to your work. They may be visible to other people using the same browser profile. Browser storage does not prevent submitted content from also being sent to AI services.

To remove the app's saved browser records, clear this site's data in your browser settings and close its tabs. This also removes saved preferences and this agreement. Clearing a draft or notebook item alone does not erase all records, and clearing browser data does not delete copies already held by a service or its logs. Ask the installation manager or service provider about those copies.

Children and learning support

A parent or legal guardian must accept for a child, supervise their use, and help them avoid sharing personal information. Guardian approval does not override a provider's age limits or terms. Only use services permitted for the learner.

AI hints, answers and learning-signal estimates can be wrong. Use them as practice support, check important results, and ask a teacher or guardian when unsure. They are not a formal assessment of a child's ability or wellbeing.
