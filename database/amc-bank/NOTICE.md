## Transcription and source credits

The processed question text and answer keys were transcribed from
**[LIVE by Po-Shen Loh](https://live.poshenloh.com/past-contests)**. Its printed
problem pages credit MAA and identify LIVE as the typesetting source. Preserve
this source credit alongside MAA's authorship credit. LIVE's own publishing
permission is not the permission relied on for this project's distribution.

The three retained original problem illustrations in 2022 AMC 8 Q25 and 2024
AMC 8 Q13 and Q19 retain their earlier Art of Problem Solving source URLs and
hashes in `Meta.FigureSources`. They are problem illustrations, not copied
third-party solution diagrams.

Copied LIVE worked solutions and their solution-only diagrams are excluded.
Nothing in this notice asserts a license to republish those explanations.
Source page links provide provenance and do not relicense the linked websites.

Eight official MAA solution booklets, preserved as retrieved, are included in
`official-solutions/`: **2018, 2019, 2020, 2022, and 2023 AMC 8**, plus
**2021 Spring, 2021 Fall, and 2022 AMC 10A**. Their original copyright and
editorial-board notices remain intact. They cover 200 questions. Two were obtained
from MAA's website and six from mirrors of MAA-authored booklets. Retrieval URLs,
file hashes, and source checks are recorded in [sources.json](sources.json).
A saved-file hash identifies the retrieved copy; publisher reference hashes
were not available for the six mirror files.

The original 200 learner-facing solutions are adapted by Math Practice from these official
booklets under the same CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 terms. Another 408 explanations adapt official MAA methods from the public sources listed in [sources.json](sources.json). No additional standalone PDFs are included. Sources outside the original eight included booklets remain linked at their hosts under their printed copyright notices and host terms.

All 608 explanations are project adaptations, not MAA-authored or MAA-reviewed explanations. [ADAPTATIONS.md](ADAPTATIONS.md) lists
the questions, source pages, modifications, and review scope. New solution
figures are cropped from official pages or drawn to support the adapted
argument; their per-record metadata identifies which.

## Original project solutions

New original project solutions, when published, are labeled **Original Math
Practice solution** and **AI-authored and independently AI-reviewed; not teacher
reviewed**. This identifies their authorship and review scope; it does not describe
them as official MAA explanations or as copied third-party solutions.

Their records retain the question's MAA attribution and transcription provenance.
Separate solution-review metadata identifies the author and reviewer models and
efforts, the reviewed solution and question hashes, and packaged review evidence.
The project source link identifies Math Practice rather than the historical
question-transcription page. These solutions remain adaptations of AMC material
under the bank's CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 terms. Official-source adaptations identify MAA and retain their source URL, page and source question number.

This provenance format supports future additions; it does not state that all
missing worked solutions have been completed or that AI review certifies their
correctness. Copied LIVE explanations and their solution-only figures remain
excluded.

## Modifications in Math Practice

We converted problem transcriptions into structured JSON and browser data,
normalized LaTeX and figure references, added stable IDs and topic metadata,
and retained reviewed corrections and grading notes. Some original geometry
figures were recovered for display. The published snapshot removes copied
third-party solution text and solution-only figures, and adds links to the eight
official MAA solution booklets, preserved as retrieved, for those questions.
The adaptations expand important deductions, explain method choices and useful
strategies, and add official-source crops or original explanatory figures.
Two prompt figures were replaced with official-source versions during the initial
six-question pilot, which is included in the 200-question batch.

Per-record metadata preserves the original source URLs, source hashes, figure
provenance, correction reasons, and modification descriptions. Historical
answer keys remain present for disputed items, which are excluded from automatic
grading. Prior processing snapshots and raw third-party website captures remain
outside this public distribution.
